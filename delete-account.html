Delete your TriviaU account

Last updated: September 4, 2026

In the app (fastest)

If you created a TriviaU account, you can delete it yourself:

  1. Open TriviaU.
  2. Tap the person icon on Home.
  3. Sign in if needed.
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

This removes your login and related profile data we control. Some gameplay records may remain in anonymized or legacy form for leaderboard integrity. Guest play does not create a TriviaU account.

Request by email

If you cannot use the in-app control, email support@tamakitech.com from the address on the account. Include the username if you remember it. We will process deletion requests sent to that address.
