Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Overview

TriviaU (“we”, “us”) provides a trivia game with optional accounts, leaderboards, groups, avatars, and a weekly poll. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

Depending on how you play, we may collect: a local player ID and display name stored on your device; optional account details (email, username, password credentials managed by Supabase Auth); game scores and leaderboard entries; group memberships; weekly poll votes; optional public avatar selections (character presets and custom photos you upload); and question or player reports you submit.

How we use information

We use this information to run gameplay, sync accounts across devices, show leaderboards and groups, display public avatars, operate the weekly poll, improve question quality, and review abuse or content reports. We do not sell your personal information.

Photos and public content

You may choose a character preset or upload a photo for your public avatar. Photo uploads are reviewed before they appear on leaderboards; until approved, your previous avatar (or preset) stays visible. Reviewed photos are stored in our cloud storage and may appear publicly on leaderboards. Choose content you are comfortable sharing. Other players may report inappropriate avatars or usernames. We may remove content that violates our terms.

Service providers

We use Supabase to provide authentication, database, and file storage services. The Android and iOS apps also use Google AdMob and Google Play Billing (or Apple In-App Purchase). Data may be processed on servers operated by those providers according to their terms and security practices.

Advertising

The Android and iOS apps use Google AdMob to show a banner on the home screen and an occasional full-screen ad after a quiz. Google and its partners may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage data to serve and measure ads. See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps. You can opt out of personalized ads at Google Ads Settings. You can pay once through the store to remove ads. The store handles that purchase. We do not see your card number.

Account deletion

If you create an account, you can delete it from the Account screen in the app. Deleting your account removes your auth account and related profile data we control. Some gameplay records may remain in anonymized or legacy form where needed for leaderboard integrity.

Guest play

You can play as a guest without creating an account. Guest progress is stored primarily on your device and may still sync scores or avatars if cloud features are enabled.

Children

TriviaU is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the TriviaU developer using the support email listed on the App Store or Google Play listing.
